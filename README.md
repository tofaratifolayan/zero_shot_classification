# zero_shot_classification

In this file, we presented our findings for detecting and resolving the uncertainty associated with zero-shot image classification. We investigated the correlation between misclassified samples and the second-to-best ratio of prediction confidence, highlighting that higher ratios strongly correlate with increased error rates. Our framework enables collaboration between models through a pipeline that generates clarifying questions, autonomously answers them, and synthesizes insights to enrich image representations. This approach yielded a 3\% improvement in classification accuracy when applied to initially misclassified images, demonstrating that our second-to-best ratio effectively identifies cases that benefit from additional information. While the optimal performance came from targeting misclassified images rather than using a fixed uncertainty threshold, our results showed that the system generates questions that effectively identify distinguishing features between competing classes. This pipeline creates a complementary relationship between AI systems of different capabilities, ultimately leading to more robust and trustworthy AI applications that can acknowledge their limitations and seek additional information when necessary.
